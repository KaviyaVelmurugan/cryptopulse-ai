# Responsible-use policy

## Intended use

CryptoPulse AI is intended for education, research, and general market-intelligence workflows. It helps users inspect crypto-news sentiment, events, data quality, evidence coverage, and historical market relationships.

## Prohibited claims

The project must not state or imply that it:

- Guarantees investment returns
- Knows the future price of an asset
- Proves that news caused a market movement
- Provides personalised investment, legal, tax, or accounting advice
- Detects manipulation or criminal conduct without appropriate verified evidence
- Is suitable for autonomous real-money trading

## Model limitations

Sentiment models can misunderstand financial language, sarcasm, quotations, negation, multilingual text, novel token names, and articles that discuss several assets. Confidence scores may be poorly calibrated and must not be presented as certainty.

## Financial limitations

Crypto markets are volatile, fragmented, continuously traded, and influenced by many variables not represented in news sentiment. Historical association can change across market regimes and does not guarantee future performance.

## Data and content

- Use provider-approved APIs and comply with rate limits and terms.
- Do not publish restricted full-text article collections.
- Preserve source links and timestamps where permitted.
- Do not collect unnecessary personal data.
- Document synthetic, licensed, and externally sourced records separately.

## Human responsibility

Users remain responsible for verifying sources, assessing risk, consulting qualified professionals, and making financial decisions. The interface must present evidence and uncertainty rather than automatic buy or sell instructions.

## Required public disclaimer

> CryptoPulse AI is an educational and research project. Its sentiment scores, market analysis, alerts, and scenarios are not financial advice and do not guarantee future results. Cryptocurrency markets involve substantial risk, including loss of capital.
